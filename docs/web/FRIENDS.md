# Using Nuvio Web on iPhone

You need an iPhone with Safari.

1. Open the Nuvio Web link in **Safari**.
2. Type your name and tap **Request access**.
3. Wait. When you're approved, Nuvio Web continues on its own to **Who's watching?**. You can leave the
   screen open, or close it and open the link again later.
4. Pick your profile.
5. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, so Nuvio opens like an app.

If you were given an invite code instead, tap **I have an invite code** on the first screen.

## Good to know

- Starting a film takes about 10 to 20 seconds while the server prepares it.
- Subtitles default to English. If they're out of time, open the subtitle menu and tap
  **Try the next best match**.
- The **ⓘ** button in the player shows playback details. A screenshot of it helps if something's wrong.
- Access can be removed at any time by the person who approved you.
- This is an independent project, not an official Nuvio app. Its source code is at the link in
  **Settings → About**.

## For the owner: approving someone

When someone asks, a red dot appears on **Settings**, and the app tells you their name.

1. Open **Settings → People** and tap **Approve** next to their name.
2. Their row changes to **Approve on Nuvio**. Tap it: Nuvio's own page opens with their code filled in.
   Tap **Approve** there as well. This lets their device use your Nuvio account. The row stays until their
   device has signed in, so you can come back to it, and it always has their latest code.

**Remove** in the same list takes their access away immediately. To also sign their device out of your
Nuvio account, remove it in Nuvio's account settings.
